About Tokyo Nepali Church

A Bible-Believing, Christ-Centered Fellowship in Tokyo, Japan

Our Story

Who We Are

Tokyo Nepali Church (TNC) is an independent, Bible-believing, Christ-centered Christian fellowship serving the Nepali community in Tokyo, Japan. We believe in the Holy Trinity — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

We exist to worship God, grow together in His Word, and reach out to every Nepali living in Japan with the love of Jesus Christ. All are welcome — believers, seekers, families, youth, and new believers.

टोक्यो नेपाली चर्च (TNC) जापानको टोक्योमा रहेको स्वतन्त्र, बाइबल-आधारित, ख्रीष्ट-केन्द्रित ख्रीष्टियन संगति हो। हामी त्रियेक परमेश्वर — पिता परमेश्वर, प्रभु येशू ख्रीष्ट र पवित्र आत्मामा पूर्ण विश्वास राख्छौं। जापानमा रहेका सबै नेपालीहरूलाई उद्धारकर्ता प्रभु येशू ख्रीष्टको सुसमाचार र प्रेम पुर्‍याउन हामी प्रतिबद्ध छौं।
Our Vision

To be a spiritual home for every Nepali in Japan — a place of worship, growth, prayer, and genuine Christian community rooted in the love of Jesus.

जापानमा रहेका प्रत्येक नेपालीको आत्मिक घर बन्नु — जहाँ बाइबलमा आधारित आराधना, आत्मिक बृद्धि, प्रार्थना र प्रभु येशू ख्रीष्टको प्रेममा जरा गाडेको सच्चा ख्रीष्टियन संगति हुन्छ।
Our Mission

To preach the Gospel, make disciples, build families in faith, and glorify God through worship, service, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

प्रभु येशूको सुसमाचार प्रचार गर्नु, शिष्यहरू बनाउनु, परिवारहरूलाई विश्वासमा निर्माण गर्नु र पवित्र आत्माको सामर्थ्यमा आराधना, प्रार्थना र सेवाद्वारा परमेश्वर पितालाई महिमा दिनु।

Our Beliefs

  • ✦ We believe in one God — Father, Son, Holy Spirit
  • ✦ The Bible is the inspired Word of God
  • ✦ Salvation through faith in Jesus Christ alone
  • ✦ The resurrection of Jesus Christ
  • ✦ Baptism by immersion in water
  • ✦ The power of the Holy Spirit
